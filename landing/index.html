How it works

Five taps from receipt to breakdown — screenshots from a real bill.

1. Photograph the receipt

2. Items are read automatically

3. Confirm tax & service

4. Tap who had what

5. Review — confirmed against the printed total

On this real receipt: subtotal 1,735.00 + 12% service 208.20 + 14% tax on the service-inclusive amount 272.05 = 2,215.25 EGP — matching the printed total exactly.
