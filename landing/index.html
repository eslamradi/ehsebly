Service is added first, then tax is charged on the larger amount. Split it the other way round and everyone at the table underpays.

How it works

Four steps from receipt to breakdown. Every screenshot below is a real bill.

1. Photograph the receipt

2. Check what it read. Every figure is editable.

3. Tap who had what

4. Everyone’s share, checked against the printed total

On this bill, subtotal 929.00 plus 14% tax 130.06 comes to 1,059.06 EGP. That matches the printed total to the piastre, then gets divided by what each person actually ordered.
