1. What ehsebly is

ehsebly reads a photographed receipt, lets you assign items to the people who ordered them, and computes each person's fair share, including their proportional share of tax and service. That's it. ehsebly is a calculation tool. It is not a payments service, not a general-purpose expense tracker beyond what Groups does for a household or a trip, and not a substitute for reading your own receipt.
